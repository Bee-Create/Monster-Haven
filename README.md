<h2>Monster Haven - Pet Monster Browser Game</h2>
<h3>View Live Page</h3>
Visit <a href="https://bee-create.github.io/Monster-Haven/">my live page</a> to see the code perform.<br>
<h3>Purpose of This Project</h3>
The purpose of this project is to practice HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript by creating an interactive browser game that let's you choose, name, and care for a pet monster. <br>
<h3>Game Features / Instructions</h3>
To start, type a name into the "name your monster" text field to give your monster a name.<br>
<hr>
If you leave the name blank, your monster will be assigned the name "Nugget" by default.<br>
<hr>
After typing in your desired name, click or tap your monster of choice: Dragon, Slime, Ghost, Bat Beast, or Mushroom Buddy.<br>
<hr>
Once you select your monster pet, the game will start.<br>
<hr>
Monitor the health, hunger, energy, and happiness bar. You can choose to:<br>
<b>-</b> Feed your monster<br>
<b>-</b> Play with your monster<br>
<b>-</b> Put your monster to sleep<br>
<b>-</b> Clean your monster<br>
<b>-</b> Give your monster medicine<br>
<hr>
Certain actions will raise the xp bar. When the xp bar fills up, your monster levels up.<br>
<hr>
You can also:
<b>-</b> See your monster's mood<br>
<b>-</b> View the event log to see your pet monster's activity<br>
<b>-</b> Navigate to the main menu<br>
<b>-</b> Choose / name a new pet<br>
<hr>
Choosing a new pet will replace the previous pet.<br>
Clsoing the game will save your current pet to come back to.
