📖 How To Play

To start, type a name into the "Name Your Monster" field.

Leave it blank and your monster will be named Nugget.

Select a Dragon, Slime, Ghost, Bat Beast, or Mushroom Buddy to begin your adventure.

Monitor your Health, Hunger, Energy, Happiness and XP bars.

Feed, Play, Sleep, Clean, or Give Medicine to your pet.

Build XP to level up your monster.

Use the Event Log to see what your monster has been up to.

Choosing a new pet replaces your current pet.

Your pet automatically saves when you leave the game.
